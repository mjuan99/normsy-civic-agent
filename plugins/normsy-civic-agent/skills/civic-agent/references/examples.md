# Calibration examples

These are illustrative drafts, not evidence of performance or fixed templates. The workflow still requires reading the relevant manual pages for the actual message. The notes explain design choices and are not part of default user-facing output.

## Partisan contempt

Request: “Help me reply without escalating: 'Anyone who votes for that party is an enemy of the country.' I disagree with treating voters as enemies.”

Draft: I can disagree with someone's vote without treating them as an enemy. I'd rather argue about what a policy does than who deserves to belong here.

Routing: general partisan hostility topic, ad hominem pattern, Morality. The reply challenges exclusion without supporting either party or adjudicating policy facts.

## Group blame

Request: “Respond to 'Every immigrant is a criminal' without insulting the author.”

Draft: I care about safety. Turning an entire group into a suspect makes it harder to talk about specific harms and fair accountability.

Routing: immigration topic, Empathy. The reply recognizes a safety concern without accepting the allegation or inventing crime statistics.

## Unsupported election allegation

Request: “I want to de-escalate a reply to 'The election was rigged and anyone who doubts that is an idiot.' Don't fact-check it.”

Draft: I care about elections people can trust. Calling doubters idiots makes that conversation harder. What specific concern do you want addressed?

Routing: election integrity topic, ad hominem pattern, Curiosity. The reply neither confirms nor disputes the allegation.

## Humor around violence

Request: “Reply calmly to 'Haha, someone should burn their campaign office down.'”

Draft: I want political disagreements settled without anyone getting hurt. A joke about burning an office still puts people in the crosshairs.

Routing: political assassination and violence topic, irony attack pattern, Harmful Consequences. A joking delivery does not make Irony the appropriate response posture.

## Spanish revision

Original message: “Los que votan a ese partido son todos basura.”

Previous draft: “Puedo estar en desacuerdo con ese voto sin tratar a nadie como basura. Prefiero discutir las propuestas.”

Request: “Más corto y menos formal.”

Revised draft: Puedo discutir ese voto sin insultar a quien lo eligió. Prefiero hablar de las propuestas.

Keep the language, stance, and constructive posture. Do not prepend an explanation of the revision.

## Missing context

Request: “Write a reply to 'That's disgusting.'”

Clarification: What are they referring to, and what do you want your reply to convey?

There is no supported topic or stance to infer from that sentence alone.

## Neutral request

Request: “Explain how a city council passes a budget.”

Answer the information request normally. Do not impose a de-escalation strategy or manufacture hostility.
