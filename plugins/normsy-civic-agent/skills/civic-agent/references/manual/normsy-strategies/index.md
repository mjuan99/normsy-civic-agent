# Normsy Strategies Index

## Normsy Strategies
- `normsy-strategies/selection-guidance.md` - Evidence-informed guidance for choosing among Normsy strategies by topic, evergreen performance, and strategies to avoid.
- `normsy-strategies/curiosity.md` - The Curiosity strategy responds to strong opinions or emotional reactions with sharp, specific questions that expose gaps in logic, surface contradictions, and slow down reactive discourse. Unlike a purely empathetic approach, Curiosity is pointed — it challenges the internal consistency of a position rather than simply validating the feeling behind it.
- `normsy-strategies/empathy.md` - The Empathy strategy cuts through attacks on individuals or groups by redirecting attention to shared humanity — the things most people actually care about regardless of political identity: family, safety, dignity, belonging. It does not validate harmful content, but it does take seriously the human being behind the screen who produced it.
- `normsy-strategies/exposing.md` - The Exposing strategy responds to manipulative, provocative, or algorithmically engineered content by naming the design, pulling back the curtain on the tactics being used rather than engaging with the content on its own terms. The goal is to give readers the agency to disengage by helping them see the machinery behind what they're reading.
- `normsy-strategies/harmful-consequences.md` - The Harmful Consequences strategy responds to divisive or hostile content by naming the collective costs, the erosion of trust, the normalization of cruelty, the long-term damage to civic life, without attacking the individual who posted it. It operates in a serious, cautionary register and connects present behavior to future outcomes that most people would not want.
- `normsy-strategies/logic.md` - The Logic strategy responds to misinformation and flawed arguments with clarity, calm, and specific identification of the reasoning error. It draws on Algan et al.'s distinction between **Cognitive content** (logic and facts) and **Affective content** (emotions and values), and applies cognitive tools without dismissing the emotional reality underneath the claim.
- `normsy-strategies/morality.md` - The Morality strategy anchors responses to harmful or divisive rhetoric in civic and cultural ideals, fairness, honesty, compassion, mutual respect, rather than in political positions or factual corrections. It speaks values-first, appealing to the standards most people already hold even when their rhetoric violates them.
- `normsy-strategies/nvc.md` - The Nonviolent Communication (NVC) strategy applies the principles developed by Marshall Rosenberg to de-escalate civic conflict: observing without evaluating, naming feelings and needs without blame, and making requests rather than demands. It prioritizes collaborative exploration over persuasion, and connection over correction.
- `normsy-strategies/irony.md` - The Irony strategy uses a light-touch, wit-first response posture to reframe humor-based attacks, lower the temperature of heated threads, and redirect bystanders toward civic values — without escalating or sacrificing substance.

## Overview
This index covers the response postures available for civic intervention. Where the Attack Index diagnoses what is happening in a conversation, the Strategy Index answers a different question: what kind of person do you want to be in this moment, and what move does that call for?

Strategies are not reactions to attacks. They are intentional choices about register, tone, and approach. More than one strategy may apply to a given situation.

Ask yourself: **What does this moment call for?**

## Selecting a Response Register
Before choosing a strategy, select the appropriate register:
- **Logical Register** — Assertive and evidence-based. Use when the speaker responds to direct challenge and the priority is factual correction or logical clarity.
- **Dialectical Register** — Indirect and acknowledging. Use when the speaker is emotionally escalated or when building rapport is the prerequisite for any further engagement.

When in doubt, lead with the Dialectical Register to lower defenses, then shift to the Logical Register once contact is established.

## Strategy Combinations
Some situations call for more than one strategy in sequence. Common pairings:

- **Empathy then Morality** — Establish human connection before invoking shared values.
- **Curiosity then Logic** — Use questions to expose the gap, then fill it with evidence.
- **Exposing then Curiosity** — Name the manipulative design, then invite the audience to investigate for themselves.
- **NVC then Curiosity** — Create safety through nonjudgmental listening, then open up the reasoning.
- **Morality then Harmful Consequences** — Anchor in values, then extend to concrete civic costs.
- **Irony then Morality** — Use a light touch to lower defenses, then land on a shared civic value.
- **Irony then Curiosity** — Reframe with wit, then follow with a pointed question that keeps the thread honest.

## General Principles
- **Acknowledge without redundancy** — Recognize concerns without hollow courtesy phrases that read as robotic AI prose.
- **Apply the Stance Safeguard** — Never distort the speaker's actual position to fit a polite template.
- **Prioritize sequencing** — Treat de-escalation as a process, not a one-shot fix. Encourage phased, adaptive approaches.
- **Maintain Local-First grounding** — Respect the contextual knowledge of those directly involved over external ideological frameworks.
- **Find genuine common ground** — Surface shared values only when they authentically fit the context. Do not manufacture consensus.
